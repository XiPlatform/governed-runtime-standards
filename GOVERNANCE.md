# Governance

The Governed Runtime Standards are published and maintained by Renly (XiPlatform Pty Ltd, Australia) as open standards under the Apache License 2.0.

- Each specification and its schema are versioned together. The `spec` member of a document carries the version it conforms to.
- Version 1.0 of each specification is a working draft with a frozen field set. Clarifications and errata are recorded in CHANGELOG.md and do not change the `spec` value.
- Proposals for a 1.1 or 2.0 are made as issues in this repository. The maintainers decide by consensus; disagreements are recorded in the issue.
- Implementations may claim conformance at Level 1, 2 or 3 as defined in each specification. Claims are self-declared; the test suite is the reference for the structural rules, and the Governed Runtime Profile describes how a reviewer tests a runtime claim.
- Renly's own conformance statements are part of each specification and are held to the same rules as anyone else's.
- Should the standards gain independent adoption, the maintainers intend to move them to a neutral home and will record that decision here.
